# Executive Findings

## What I Analyzed

I modeled 12,339 invoice-line spend records, 119 suppliers, 2,142 supplier scorecard rows, 29 sourcing bids, and 10 category baselines for a freight rail category-management workflow.

## Findings

- The modeled spend base is $2,079,713,349, with $590,147,308 classified as off-contract, expired-agreement, or catalog-mismatch leakage.
- The top category priority is Diesel Fuel and Lubricants with a criticality score of 58.9, 14.7% non-compliant spend, and $15,521,685 in modeled savings potential.
- The strongest sourcing scenario is Locomotive Components with Mechanical Supplier 1, a weighted score of 80.5, and $1,008,001 modeled savings versus baseline.
- The highest supplier follow-up risk is Indirect Supplier 5 in MRO Tools and Shop Supplies with a risk score of 30.3 and current status of Watch.

## Recommendation

Use the criticality queue to choose the next sourcing wave, use the sourcing studio to pressure-test supplier award options, and use the scorecard monitor to separate negotiation opportunities from supplier recovery work.
